Ask for tenure reduction in writing. After a prepayment, a lender can either keep your EMI and shorten the loan, or keep the loan length and shrink your EMI. The money involved is not close, and several lenders default to the second unless you specify otherwise.

Reducing the EMI frees up cash every month, which is a real benefit if the budget is tight. But it keeps the balance earning interest for the full original term, and that is where the difference goes.

Paying now vs. after the next EMI

Why this differs from other calculators

The common shortcut is months saved × EMI − prepayment, which assumes both schedules end on a full instalment. They don't — each ends on a stub, and the two stubs aren't the same size.
